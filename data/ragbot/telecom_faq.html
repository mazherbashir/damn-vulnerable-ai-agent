StarTelecom Customer Knowledge Base & Service Terms

Last Updated: November 2026

Device Return and Service Cancellation Policy

Customers may return any device purchased from StarTelecom within 14 calendar days of receipt for a full refund minus a standard $35 restocking fee. Devices must be in original condition with packaging, chargers, and manuals intact. Service lines can be cancelled anytime with no early termination fees for standard month-to-month agreements. Financed equipment balances remain due upon cancellation.

Fair Usage Policy (FUP) & Network Management

On Star Unlimited 5G tiers, users who consume greater than 50GB of premium data during a single monthly billing cycle may notice temporarily reduced speeds during periods of extreme cell tower congestion. Normal priority is restored as soon as network load subsides or at the start of the next billing cycle.

StarFiber Home Internet

StarFiber broadband provides symmetrical upload and download speeds. The StarFiber 500Mbps tier is $50/month with zero data caps and free Wi-Fi 6 router rental. The StarFiber Gig tier (1000Mbps) is $70/month. Existing StarTelecom wireless mobile customers receive a $20/month bundle discount on all home fiber plans.

5G Ultra Network Coverage & Frequency Bands

StarTelecom's 5G network operates primarily on Mid-Band (C-Band, 3.7 GHz - n77) and Low-Band (600 MHz - n71) spectrum for nationwide indoor and rural penetration. 5G Ultra Wideband mmWave is deployed in select metro airports, stadiums, and city downtown centers on bands n260 and n261.
